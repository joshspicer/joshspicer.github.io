Boston Calling is an incredibly special event for me annually. For several years, this weekend held some of my fondest college memories, and I think the festival embodies how awesome a city Boston is. Boston Calling also has the ability to promote local indie artists in a powerful way - something I wanted to be a part of.

I grew up right outside Boston and went to school at Northeastern. When I wasn't in class studying computer science, I was at the Royale, Brighton Music Hall, or a house show on Mass Ave with friends enjoying the best part of living in Boston: the music. I averaged about three shows a month that semester, and it still didn't seem like enough.

What Boston Calling does is bring this amazing music culture out into the open for all Bostonians to enjoy. The festival gets better each year at providing a platform not only for giant artists, but also for smaller independent artists to get the recognition they deserve.

One great example that year was artist Sidney Gish. Sidney was a former roommate of a good friend, and I had met her and fervently listened to her music. She is an amazingly talented artist, and I was excited that Boston Calling gave her a platform to share her music with such a large audience.

Being part of the staff would have been an amazing experience and a way to help create and share my love for the festival.
