Originally a Northeastern polls server, SpiceCraft became a Minecraft server for friends and community members.

Players are expected to be kind to one another and respectful of each other's builds.

The server uses a whitelist. [Contact me](/contact/) if you would like to ask about joining, and check the [live map](http://mc.joshspicer.com:8123).

You can also visit the [server Discord](https://discord.gg/b9HJEVH).

**Server address:** `mc.spicer.dev`
