I'm **Josh**, a graduate of Northeastern University's [Khoury College of Computer Sciences](https://ccs.neu.edu/home/joshua).

I joined Microsoft in 2020 on the Xamarin tooling team. Before that, I interned in Microsoft's Intelligent Conversation and Communication Cloud group.

I abide by the three C's: **concerts, coffee, and [cats](/cinnamon/)**.

## What I've done

I previously worked as a **security consultant** at Virtual Security Research, performing application, network, and mobile penetration testing for client companies.

I also worked at [Akamai Technologies](https://akamai.com/) on its Enterprise Network Engineering team.

I lived briefly in [Florence, Italy](/geo-fellowship/) and later in [Edinburgh, UK](/hitch-to-paris/), where I studied abroad at the University of Edinburgh.

## What else did I do?

I helped develop **Parade**, an event-discovery platform for Northeastern clubs that was featured by [News@Northeastern](https://news.northeastern.edu/2019/07/23/want-to-know-whats-happening-on-northeasterns-boston-campus-theres-an-app-for-that/).

I spent plenty of time constructing elaborate [Spotify playlists](/monthly-playlist-2018/) and buying concert tickets.

I participated on Northeastern's Collegiate Cyber Defense Team, helped produce Survivor Northeastern, and founded the Computer Science Mentoring Organization (CoSMO), which ran technical workshops and student-driven panels.
